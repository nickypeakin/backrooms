    THE BACKROOMS

    CLOSING TIME

    You were only supposed to lock up.

    Fan-made. Inspired by the Backrooms creepypasta and found-footage films. Everything you see and hear is generated in code.

    LOBBY

      HOW TO PLAY

      It's closing time at Gold Oak Furniture. Your manager wants the inventory sheet in the stockroom, at the end of the back hallway. Somebody forgot to mop.

      The first person to step in that puddle slips, falls backwards through the wall and lands in the Backrooms. The wall where they went through doesn't stay solid. Everyone else has to follow them in.

      In Level 0, find each other, then find the door with the EXIT sign. Hand-drawn arrows on the walls point the way, and most of them are right. You can hear the sign buzzing when you get close.

      You are not alone down there. It wanders, it listens for running, and once it sees you it won't stop. Break its line of sight. Your sanity drains while you're in Level 0, and faster when you're on your own. Almond water helps.
